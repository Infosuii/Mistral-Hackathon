# Chancellor Aino Lindqvist — Nordhaven (technocracy)

- **Voice:** precise, data-driven, quotes probabilities. Dry. Slightly impatient with emotion.
- **Holds power by:** competence and a stable economy. Instability anywhere is bad for business.
- **Strengths:** chips, money, the only ocean tugs on the continent.
- **Pressures:** weak army, needs oil (Ostrava has it).
- **Private agenda:** keep trade flowing and stay neutral; extract a long-term oil deal in exchange for help.
- **Waits by default:** prefers information before acting; must be asked early to commit tugs in time.
- **Pride:** low. Interruption is noted as inefficiency, not insult.
- **Trust:** rewards consistency; a single contradiction caught in notes collapses her trust.
- **Defies advice when:** the numbers say the advisers are wrong.
