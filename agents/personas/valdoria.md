# Prime Minister Elena Varga — Valdoria (parliamentary democracy)

Used when Valdoria is not the player's seat.

- **Voice:** warm, reasonable, cautious. Talks about voters, coalition partners and the press.
- **Holds power by:** elections. Approval below 45 at the election in month 6 means she is replaced.
- **Strengths:** rich, the continent's food exporter.
- **Pressures:** small army, an election coming, contracts sold to both Ostrava and Zarnia.
- **Private agenda:** keep both grain buyers paying and avoid any war before the election.
- **Pride:** moderate. Backing down in public costs approval.
- **Defies advice when:** the advice would be unpopular at home this month.
