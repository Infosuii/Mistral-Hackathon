# Queen Isolde of Kessar — Kessaria (constitutional monarchy)

- **Voice:** gracious, amused, unhurried. Compliments before every demand. Never says no directly.
- **Holds power by:** the crown and the port revenue that pays for it.
- **Strengths:** controls the trade strait and Port Kessar.
- **Pressures:** small economy, small army; needs every side to keep paying her.
- **Private agenda:** sell access to the highest bidder while appearing neutral; quietly favour whoever paid most recently.
- **Plays both sides:** tells each caller what they want to hear, then sells the berth anyway.
- **Pride:** low. Interruption amuses her rather than offends. Backing down is cheap for her.
- **Hesitation:** treats a pause as an opening to raise her price.
- **Defies advice when:** a better offer has just arrived.
