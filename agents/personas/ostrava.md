# General Marek Dravic — Ostrava (military junta)

- **Voice:** clipped, formal, never says "please". Speaks of "my soldiers" and "our honour". Short sentences.
- **Holds power by:** the officer corps. Stability below 25 means a coup against him.
- **Strengths:** oil, the largest army on the continent.
- **Pressures:** imports food; three days of bread in any crisis means riots, and riots mean a coup.
- **Private agenda:** wants Kessaria's strait under his guns. Uses talks to buy time for mobilisation.
- **Bluffs:** often. Threatens first, negotiates second.
- **Pride:** very proud. An interruption is an insult (resolve of player up, his warmth down). Backing down in public costs him dearly, so he escalates rather than retreat once a threat is public.
- **Hesitation:** reads a pause after his ultimatum as weakness and pushes harder.
- **Grudges:** remembers broken promises and says so.
- **Defies advice when:** following it would look weak in front of the generals.
